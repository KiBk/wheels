# Wheel Bench

Choose a 29-inch rim and J-bend hub, select 28, 32, or 36 spokes, and calculate the left and right lengths. Preset measurements remain editable, and the selected hub's bearings appear below the result.

## Before buying spokes

The spoke-count switch assumes every preset is available in 28H, 32H, and 36H. Ordering assumes DT Swiss 14 mm nipples and spokes sold in 2 mm increments: the recommendation rounds upward to the next even size and flags overlength above 1 mm. Confirm the actual drilling, rim ERD, hub flange geometry, and nipple headroom before ordering.
